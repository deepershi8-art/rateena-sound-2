# Editorial visual edition

11 November 2026 — Africa/Cairo.

The owner's visual feedback was applied without changing engineering content, app hierarchy, room data, persistence keys, technical calculations or the supplied logo.

## Visual changes

- Architectural-monograph / luxury-audio direction rather than dashboard cards.
- Large, tightly tracked Cormorant Garamond display headlines with Inter body copy and small letter-spaced labels.
- Near-black #07090d, a sparing metallic-gold headline gradient, steel blue for Phase Two, cyan for technical sketches.
- Square hairline frames, generous negative space, tall image-free facility panels and oversized editorial numerals.
- Slim underlined tabs, numbered book spreads and a ten-section editorial Technical Study index.
- Restrained fade/slide reveals, drawn hover underlines, subtle static grain and reduced-motion support.
- The drafting canvas stays empty and unobstructed.

## Review iterations

The production build was screenshot-tested, then refined for stronger small-text contrast, larger explanatory text and a clearer first fold showing facility names. Desktop, mobile, full-page and static-comparison screenshots were inspected. A native-resolution crop also confirmed the app-bar title renders correctly.

Bundled Playwright Chromium succeeded; the Xvfb and stock-Chrome fallbacks were not required. Screenshot motion is paused for consistent final-state images. The application itself retains normal restrained motion.
