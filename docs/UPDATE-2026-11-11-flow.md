## Implementation brief (as relayed)

Restructure the navigation flow of the existing app. Keep the editorial navy/gold design system, official logo, existing content, true-scale plan, room side panels, Center placeholders and localStorage data. Reuse, move or hide content; do not delete it.

1. **Splash** — only the official logo, large, full-screen dark background, no motion, at most a subtle "Tap anywhere" hint. Tap/click anywhere (Enter/Space) continues.
2. **Gateway** — two options: Studio Management and RATEENA SOUND.
3. **Studio Management** — only the studio graphic plan (owner upload pending). Full-bleed slot "Studio graphic plan – to be uploaded" with local image upload persisted in the browser. Ready for future room hotspots; no fake management data. Old Project Management content retired from navigation but kept in code/docs.
4. **RATEENA SOUND** — RATEENA SOUND Flagship Studio (gold) and RATEENA SOUND Center (blue). Center keeps expandable placeholders, worded to host the other types of studios and facilities.
5. **Flagship Studio** — tabs Knowledge and Technical Study. Technical Study opens with a short design brief, then the 2D plan (true-scale interactive plan plus an owner 2D plan upload slot), then room panels and existing study sections. Design brief: proportions informed by golden-ratio proportioning (not exact; show actual ratio normalised to height); live room sized for up to 25 musicians (design target, supersedes 12–15); length lets bass wavelengths develop (low E ≈ 41 Hz → ≈ 8.3–8.4 m at 343 m/s, calculated); height lets upper frequencies and early reflections sound natural; control room central with glazing to live room, lounge, percussion booth and bass/vocal booths — add proposed double/laminated glazing symbols to the true-scale plan, no invented ratings.
6. **Knowledge order** — Sound; Stopping sound / isolation (mass, decoupling, floating construction, box-in-box; keep 3-stage model; image slot per topic); Acoustic treatment with an illustrative interactive 3D wireframe cube (orbitable, constant source, reflecting wavefronts/rays, standing waves with nodes/antinodes, L/W/H sliders defaulting to 12.00/8.33/4.50, first axial modes f = n·c/2L per dimension, coincident/clustered modes highlighted, spacing quality; reduced-motion respected with pause; mobile-friendly); then HVAC/conditioning, equipment infrastructure and equipment (short chapters / links into Technical Study). Terminology and standards kept as appendices.
7. No fabricated specs, no dead buttons, keep true scale.
