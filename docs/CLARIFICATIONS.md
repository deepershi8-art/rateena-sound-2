### True-scale spatial study, not an approved building

All plan geometry uses metres with uniform screen projection. Rooms are not shrunk to fit the contradictory concept envelope. The expanded arrangement retains: Live across the top; Equipment → Percussion → Control → stacked Bass/Vocal → Storage in the middle; Lounge/Services below, with WC Men/Women bottom left and entrance bottom centre.

- Equipment 2.00 × 4.00 m and Storage 2.00 × 5.00 m are clearly labelled **area-derived diagram assumptions** because only their areas were supplied. They are editable, not approved dimensions.
- Nominal 0.30 m wall zones are a **Proposed drawing allowance**, not an engineered isolation assembly. Independent leaves/cavities/structure remain pending.
- A typical 1.20 m access strip east of the booths and below the middle band is an explicit diagram assumption outside room net areas. This is not a circulation/accessibility/fire approval. The drawing extent is not declared to be a building footprint or gross building area.
- Doors are typical 0.90 m leaves, with a 1.80 m entrance pair. Locations and swings are proposed and require coordination. WC cubicles are typical 1.50 × 0.90 m references, included in Lounge/Services; no accessible-toilet compliance is implied.
- Optional objects are **typical, not selected equipment**: concert grand 2.74 × 1.57 m; kit 1.8 × 1.5 m; riser 2.4 × 2.4 m; double-bass player 1 × 1 m (the instrument’s approximately 1.9 m height is not a plan width); console 2.2 × 1.0 m; engineer chair diameter 0.65 m; sofa 2.1 × 0.9 m; rack enclosure 0.6 × 1.0 m; musician chair space 0.8 × 0.8 m.
- A full concert grand is shown only in the Live Room. A 3 × 2.5 m booth has only 0.26 m spare along its 3 m axis before clearances; in the shown orientation the 2.74 m length exceeds the 2.50 m depth. No viable piano-booth layout is claimed.
- Footprint containment checks are not circulation, acoustic or operational validation. Edited dimensions flag objects that no longer fit; objects are never automatically scaled down.
- SVG export uses physical millimetre dimensions at 1:50, 1:100 or 1:200. The UI reports whether the current sheet fits A3 at 100% printing. Screen zoom is not a physical scale. Disable printer “fit to page”.

### Evidence and preservation

Schema v2 stores a new eight-room concept programme separately from the original five-room study. All existing edited rooms, decisions, targets, references and history remain. Old browser storage is left intact as a backup; v1 imports are upgraded rather than reset. Actual study notes and attachments remain local unless the owner exports them; they are not automatically pushed into the public repository.

Room-in-Room is the studio-wide intent; Mass–Decoupling–Damping and floating construction are complementary. Wall/floor/ceiling principles are separate. Support types, loads, resonance, cavity depths and material build-ups require engineering; no universal springs or shared isolated raft are assumed.

IEC 61672-1 / IEC 61260-1 describe relevant measurement instrumentation; ITU-R BS.1116 concerns subjective assessment of small audio impairments; EBU Tech 3276 concerns listening conditions.
